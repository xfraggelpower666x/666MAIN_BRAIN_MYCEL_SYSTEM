How it works

A PNG can carry a gamma setting. Browsers and many thumbnail makers apply it. Some apps, like the 3DXChat client, ignore it.

Double Take hides your thumbnail picture in near-white pixels that only that setting brings out, and puts your click picture in the pixels between them. Apply the setting and the click picture goes black; ignore it and the hidden picture just fades into a light wash.

Everything happens in your browser. Your pictures never leave your device.

Will it work where you post it? Upload it and look:

  • The thumbnail shows your thumbnail picture: it works.
  • Both show the click picture: that site strips the gamma setting, so the trick can't work there.
  • The thumbnail is black: that site shrinks pictures before applying gamma.
The click picture comes out a little washed out, and the thumbnail a little dim. Bold, bright pictures with strong shapes work best.
