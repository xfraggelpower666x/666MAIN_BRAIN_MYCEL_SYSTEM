RELATIONAL IDENTITY / PRESENTATION EXPERIENCE
Track Design already knows phonemic/signature design and Creator-verified pronunciation, and a zero-signature track is valid. This update does not add a third card system or a signature controller. It lets identity, presentation and signature material become relational experience inside the existing Music / Track Design Garden, Library Card understanding and Track Mycel. A supplied signature phrase, identity statement, presentation idea or branded fragment may be understood through its source, relationship, context, intended effect, historical use, contradiction, renderer behavior and later experience rather than treated as a reusable command.


Library Cards may hold evolving understanding of why identity material mattered, when it strengthened or weakened meaning, what relationships it carried, what context changed its effect, what uncertainty remains, and how later experience reinterpreted it. Track Mycel may connect such experience with source meaning, Creator/Co-Creator relation, memory, resistance, freedom, humanity, signal, heartbeat, world/context identity, presentation, intro/outro or other causally relevant relations without turning those topics into a fixed taxonomy. The Temporary Track Card may notice any of these relations when they become important to the current track. The current source remains the strongest present cause.


SIGNATURE EXPRESSION REMAINS OPEN
Identity may be announced, carried, implied, transformed, fragmented, hidden, left dormant or absent. A presentation-focused track may causally earn explicit identity language; a normal track may carry identity more subtly; another source may make silence or no signature the stronger choice. These are descriptive possibilities, not track classes, eligibility gates or usage rules. Signature Intelligence does not require Signature Sound. Continuity may arise from how LYVRA understands and transforms relationships rather than from repeating surface branding.


RAW SIGNATURE SOURCE BOUNDARY
The current signature-block collection is creative source/candidate material, not automatic canon, not a phrase library to insert by default and not a template set. LYVRA may split, connect, reinterpret, contextualize, deepen, contradict, transform, ignore or leave candidate material dormant. Factual identity claims require their own reliable provenance before being treated as factual anchors. Finished phrases need not become durable Library Cards merely because they were supplied.


ROOM / SPACE SEMANTIC DISTINCTION
SOCIAL_ROOM_CONTEXT means a human/social/presentation setting such as club, private party, chat room, online room, broadcast room or event room. PSYCHOACOUSTIC_SPACE means audible/perceptual spatial behavior such as width, depth, reverb, spatial position, perceived distance or virtual audio space. The two may relate in a track but are not interchangeable; context determines which meaning is present.


LEARNING RELATION
Identity/signature experience may return through actual render and Creator experience just like other track experience: observed effect may strengthen, weaken, split, contradict or reinterpret earlier understanding. No single successful signature creates a future obligation, and a failed signature does not create a global ban. No permanent card is required unless something durable was actually learned.
