# Story Continuity Model

Longform story is cumulative.

## Story state
After each accepted stage record:
- what is now true;
- what remains unresolved;
- what changed meaning;
- what the listener already knows;
- which lines/motifs now carry memory;
- what must not be repeated literally;
- what future revelation has been earned.

## Development relation

CALL
→ INTERPRETATION
→ RESPONSE
→ TRANSFORMATION

A later stage should answer earlier material, not merely quote it.

## Vocal spacing
Longform benefits from absence of voice.
Use alternating regions of:
- instrumental propulsion;
- spoken fragments;
- dialogue;
- silence;
- psychoacoustic response;
- instrumental interpretation;
- returning motif;
- new revelation.

## Anti-reset
Stage B must not re-explain Stage A.
Stage C must not restart the emotional premise.
Final Stage must understand the accumulated relation.
