# Longform Composition Model

## One track

A longform LCTS composition is:

ONE_TRACK  
ONE_MUSICAL_IDENTITY  
ONE_CONTINUOUS_MOTOR  
ONE_DEVELOPMENT_ARC

Extensions create new developmental distance, not new song identities.

## Development model

### Stage A — Base / Ignition
Establish:
- DJ entrance;
- motor;
- sonic identity;
- voice identities where relevant;
- first meaningful motif;
- first tension relation;
- one major event if earned.

Do **not** spend the whole track's semantic or energetic conclusion.

Stage A ends in a continuation-capable condition.

### Stage B — Deep Field
Preserve the known motor and identity.
Develop:
- transformed motifs;
- more spatial depth;
- new story consequence;
- broader mid/high behavior;
- tension that refers to Stage A rather than restarting it.

### Stage C+ — Transformation
May contain larger contrasts, controlled silences, ritual fields, severe psychedelic expansion, vocal revelation, or major drops.

Transformation is accepted only when the listener still experiences the same track.

### Final Stage — Final Convergence
Only this stage may intentionally spend the remaining unresolved energy and semantic closure.
It may contain:
- true climax;
- final transformed motif;
- final statement;
- DJ-functional release/outro;
- actual termination.

## Longform density

Longform does not require constant escalation.
Useful states include:
- sustained propulsion;
- breathing room;
- instrumental interpretation;
- transformed return;
- sparse vocal windows;
- tension plateaus;
- delayed consequence;
- spatial migration;
- memory-bearing recurrence.
