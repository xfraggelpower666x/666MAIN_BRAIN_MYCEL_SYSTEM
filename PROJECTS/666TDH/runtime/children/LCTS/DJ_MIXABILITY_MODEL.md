# DJ Mixability Model

LCTS treats the final Whole Song as a DJ object, not only a listening journey.

## Entrance
The Base should provide enough readable motor before identity becomes too dense:
- clear downbeat;
- stable 4/4 reference;
- known kick/bass relationship;
- phrase boundaries;
- no immediate deceptive low-end modulation.

## Through-track readability
The DJ should be able to trust:
- BPM;
- kick spacing;
- bass pulse;
- bar count;
- major phrase arrivals.

Creative disorientation is encouraged mainly above that frame.

## Extension joins
A join is DJ-safe when it does not create:
- tempo wobble;
- bar displacement;
- new groove identity;
- bass phase surprise severe enough to feel like a new track;
- sudden intro restart;
- artificial end/start seam.

## Exit
Only Final Stage plans the true mix-out region.
The outro may reduce life while retaining temporal legibility long enough for mixing.

## DJ review questions
1. Can I grid this track without chasing the beat?
2. Do kick and bass remain temporally trustworthy after every Extend?
3. Does any join feel like a second song starting?
4. Are phrase boundaries still inferable under psychedelic density?
5. Is the outro actually usable rather than merely quiet?
