## Longform rule
Do not deploy every capability early.

A spatial behavior becomes meaningful when it has history.
Example:
1. Stage A introduces a centered motif.
2. Stage B sends transformed shadows around it.
3. Stage C breaks or reverses its orbit.
4. Final Stage may converge it or leave it suspended, depending on meaning.

Complexity gains force through accumulated relation, not feature count.
