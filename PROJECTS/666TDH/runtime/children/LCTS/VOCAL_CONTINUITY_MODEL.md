# Vocal Continuity Model

## Voice identity
For each named voice record:
- role;
- register;
- distance/intimacy;
- articulation;
- emotional restraint/intensity;
- relationship to other voices.

## Continuity
An extension should preserve recognizable voice identity unless the story itself transforms that voice.

## Return with history
Repeated lines should gain changed function through:
- different speaker;
- changed spatial relation;
- changed context;
- changed pressure;
- reduced wording;
- transformed response.

## Avoid
- permanent narration;
- explanation of already-known facts;
- random speaker changes;
- chorus reset at every extension;
- vocal ending gestures before a planned continuation.
