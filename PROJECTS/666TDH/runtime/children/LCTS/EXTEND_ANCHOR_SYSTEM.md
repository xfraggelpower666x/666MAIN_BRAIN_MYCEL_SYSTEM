# Extend Anchor System

## Preferred anchor

A strong Extend anchor sits inside a stable phrase rather than after a conclusion.

Preferred conditions:
- kick running;
- bass running;
- established low-end relation;
- no fill ending;
- no completed drop tail;
- no vocal final sentence;
- no fade;
- no cadence;
- minimal semantic load;
- upper field unresolved;
- forward motion obvious.

## Anchor record

Each anchor stores:
- source Whole Song version;
- approximate timestamp/window;
- bar/phrase context if known;
- motor confidence;
- semantic load;
- transition risk;
- intended next development.

## Anchor quality

### GREEN
Stable propulsion, neutral semantic content, no closure signal.

### AMBER
Usable but contains a fill, vocal fragment, or active transformation that could bias the continuation.

### RED
Ending gesture, silence after closure, outro, cadence, tempo instability, or identity reset risk.

## Principle

The perfect anchor is not necessarily musically exciting.
It is musically **open**.
