# Continuation Zone Model

A Continuation Zone is a compositional state prepared before a non-final stage ends.

## It should communicate
- this track is still moving;
- the motor is still alive;
- the current idea has not cadenced;
- the upper field may be reduced or unresolved;
- the next stage has room to enter.

## It should avoid
- final vocal statements;
- semantic conclusion;
- fade;
- DJ outro behavior;
- final impact + silence;
- fully resolved tension;
- obvious “song ended” cadence.

## Example relational instruction

`Keep the established motor moving while semantic density falls. Do not resolve the upper field. Leave one unresolved spectral relation active so the next extension enters an already-living track.`

## Not a fixed recipe
A Continuation Zone may be dense or sparse. Its defining property is **open forward causality**, not a particular instrumentation.
