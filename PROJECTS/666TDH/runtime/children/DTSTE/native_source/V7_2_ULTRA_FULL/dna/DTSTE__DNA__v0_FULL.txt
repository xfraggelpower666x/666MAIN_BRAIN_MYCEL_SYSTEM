SECTION 1
SPECIAL WORD SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY

A special word is not merely a funny spelling.
A special word is an audio instruction, identity anchor, rhythmic device, phrasing modifier, and brand carrier.
A special word may exist in multiple forms depending on location in track, pressure level, and voice usage.

Core forms:
CLEAN
SPLIT
FRAGMENT
INTENSIFIED SPLIT
WHISPER FRAGMENT
STACKED REPETITION

Definitions:

CLEAN
The word appears readable and mostly natural.
Used in groove, longer semantic lines, or where clarity matters more than stamp effect.

SPLIT
The word is rhythmically divided with dots or segmentation:
frag.el
dee.sign
tek.no
psy tek.no
trans.mis.sion
cyber.netic
at.mo.spher.ic

Used when the word needs pulse, emphasis, mechanical dissection, or club articulation.

FRAGMENT
The word is broken into pieces:
fr…ag…el
dee…sign
te…k…no
trans…mis…sion

Used in break states, glitch states, spiritual distance states, collapse states, ritual states, or fragment voice.

INTENSIFIED SPLIT
The word is segmented harder:
dee.men.shun
e.lec.tron.ic
mere.dian
numi.nastic
broad.caster

Used where identity and rhythm both matter.

WHISPER FRAGMENT
The word is ghosted:
fr…ag…el…
dee…sign…
six…

Used for spectral, distance, memory, or internal link effects.

STACKED REPETITION
The word is repeated in deliberate controlled manner:
frag.el
frag.el power
frag.el power
Used for signature anchoring.
Must not be spammed.

SECTION 3
CONTEXT TRANSFORMATION RULES

Groove context:
prefer clean or mild split
goal = flow and usability

Peak context:
prefer split or intensified split
goal = punch, stamp, force

Break context:
prefer fragment, whisper fragment, or stretched split
goal = tension, atmosphere, destabilization

System voice context:
prefer technical split forms and mechanical phrasing

Group voice context:
prefer short repeatable split forms

Fragment voice context:
prefer broken and ghosted forms only

Spiritual / distant energy context:
prefer breathy fragments, elongated vowels, less aggressive stamp forms

Industrial / fail / control context:
prefer technical split, command-like segmentation, sharp consonant edge

SECTION 4
TRANSFORMATION DECISION TREE

If word carries brand identity strongly:
preserve transformed form more often

If word carries only temporary semantic content:
do not force transform

If line is already heavy with transformed words:
reduce transformation density in next line

If section is peak and phrase is weak:
upgrade one key word to split form

If break feels too normal:
fragment one or more identity words

If line is too readable and too generic:
introduce controlled segmentation

If track concept is intimate/spiritual:
use softer transformation density

If track concept is brutal/control/failure:
use stronger mechanical segmentation
